Chenliang Xu is a tenured Associate Professor of Computer Science at the University of Rochester. His research lies at the intersection of computer vision, audio-visual learning, and trustworthy AI, with a focus on teaching machines to understand the world through video, sound, and language. He has published over 130 papers at top venues including CVPR, NeurIPS, ICCV, ECCV, ICLR, and ICML, with support from agencies such as DARPA, NSF, and NIH. His work has received multiple best paper awards, and he has served as an area chair for major conferences in computer vision and machine learning.
